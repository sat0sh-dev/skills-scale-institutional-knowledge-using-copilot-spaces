# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme delivers cross-functional projects through an iterative lifecycle: initiation validates the need and aligns stakeholders; planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized backlog items, milestones, dependencies, and clear quality expectations; execution delivers small increments through a project board, reviewed pull requests, testing, demos, and regular progress tracking; and release, retrospective, and continuous improvement complete the cycle. Risk registers, escalation paths, status updates, and a shared source of truth keep the team aligned and ensure blockers are addressed promptly.

The core roles are the Project Manager, who coordinates delivery and communication; the Product Manager, who defines outcomes and prioritizes the backlog; developers and QA/testing, who design, build, test, and validate work; and stakeholders, who provide input and approvals. Key artifacts include the project one-pager, roadmap and release plan, sprint backlog, acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, risk register, and retrospective action items.

Quality is built into delivery: CI runs tests, linting, and security scans; pull requests stay small and include issue links and acceptance criteria; at least one approval is required before merging; and unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual testing are used according to risk and feature requirements. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, rollback or mitigation plans, staged smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, incidents, or milestones convert learnings into owned, time-bound improvement actions.
