# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Leads / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Testing Specialists own the quality strategy and help teams establish effective validation throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release-readiness criteria
- Establish and maintain automated and manual test coverage
- Review acceptance criteria and identify testability gaps
- Report defects, quality trends, and residual risk

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on test automation, testability, and defect resolution.
- Partners with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on test plans, release readiness, dependencies, and quality risks.

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads connect user needs, product outcomes, and implementable experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Lead discovery, interaction design, and usability validation
- Define design patterns and accessibility expectations
- Facilitate feedback and validate proposed solutions with users
- Provide implementation specifications and review delivered experiences

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to frame problems, explore options, and align designs with success metrics.
- Collaborates with Developers on feasible implementation and design reviews.
- Provides Project Managers with design dependencies, review milestones, and validation status.

---

## Security Leads

### Role Summary
Security Leads establish security requirements, assess risk, and support secure, compliant delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements
- Review high-risk designs and changes
- Coordinate vulnerability triage and incident response
- Recommend mitigations and track security risks to resolution

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation and reviews technical risks.
- Partners with Product Managers to account for security constraints in prioritization and scope.
- Gives Project Managers visibility into security dependencies, gates, and escalation needs.

---

## Release / DevOps Leads

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Leads own repeatable, observable, and reversible delivery to production.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, environments, and release automation
- Plan deployment windows and rollback or mitigation steps
- Monitor release health and post-deployment verification
- Coordinate operational readiness and release communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on build, deployment, and observability requirements.
- Coordinates with QA Leads on pre-release and smoke-testing gates.
- Aligns Product Managers on release scope and impact and Project Managers on schedules, dependencies, and status.

---

## Support / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Leads represent operational readiness and customer experience after and during delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Assess support and operational impacts before release
- Prepare documentation, runbooks, and escalation paths
- Triage production issues and communicate known impacts
- Feed recurring incidents and user feedback into planning and retrospectives

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Helps Developers and Product Managers understand operational constraints and recurring customer issues.
- Gives Project Managers actionable risks, support needs, and release communications.
- Participates with QA Leads in defining production checks and acceptance for operational readiness.

---

## Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, decision authority, funding, and organizational support for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and approve major scope or investment decisions
- Resolve high-impact escalations and remove organizational barriers
- Review outcomes and benefits at key decision gates
- Support adoption and cross-functional alignment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers on strategic outcomes, prioritization, and major trade-offs.
- Provides Project Managers with executive decisions and escalation support.
- Receives delivery and risk updates from Project Managers and outcome measures from Product Managers.

---

## Role Assignment Guidance

A project does not need a dedicated person for every persona. One person may cover more than one role, and external specialists may contribute at specific gates. The Project Manager should document named owners or shared accountabilities for quality, design, security, release, operations, and executive decisions during planning so that handoffs and escalations remain clear.
